Resilient Advisor

A cross-functional partner for executives.

The Resilient Advisor operates at the intersection of executive leadership, Cyber, IT, Risk, Compliance, AI, Procurement and Business functions.

The Resilient Advisor does not replace the CISO, CTO, COO or Chief Risk Officer, but gives them a consolidated view of actual resilience across functions.

The role brings together expertise and dashboards to create a shared picture that decision-makers can act on.

Cash management

AUSECAF

AUSECAF is dedicated to securing and controlling cash management activities.

Its framework covers access rights, payments, flows, banking interfaces, fraud, traceability and business continuity.

Objective: provide a clear view of control maturity and priority actions.

Yet the administration of cash management applications is still rarely audited.

Banking data exchanges

SECEDI

SECEDI secures data exchanges, processes and transactions, including those using the EBICS TS protocol.

The solution governs the administration of signatories, their activation or revocation, and the management and traceability of their authentication tokens.

The solution supports risk identification, control design, evidence verification and remediation tracking.

Most organisations outsource this sensitive administration: SECEDI enables them to control its security, compliance and traceability.

Objective: retain control of signing rights and ensure the long-term security of sensitive banking data exchanges.

Cybersecurity & compliance

CIATA Manager

CIATA Manager is a unique, proprietary consulting service for managing cyber resilience through our exclusive CIATA methodology (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, Trust and Authenticity).

Our approach incorporates the essential pillar of Authenticity required by DORA, an area where most organisations still fall short. It also ensures that all these controls are automated and run continuously.

Objective: scale up risk monitoring and replace manual audits with automated controls that run continuously, using our exclusive methodological framework.
