Resilient Advisor

A cross-functional partner for executives.

The Resilient Advisor operates at the intersection of executive leadership, Cyber, IT, Risk, Compliance, AI, Procurement and Business functions.

It does not replace the CISO, CTO, COO or Chief Risk Officer. It provides them with a consolidated, cross-functional view of actual resilience.

It turns a collection of expertise and dashboards into a shared view that decision-makers can act on directly.

Explore the Resilient Advisor →

Responsibilities

  • Consolidate cyber, technology, operational and AI risks
  • Track controls and supporting evidence
  • Identify critical non-conformities
  • Manage remediation plans
  • Track resilience testing
  • Prepare trade-offs and decisions
  • Improve executive reporting
Proprietary solutions

Specialised frameworks and methods.

Cash management

AUSECAF

AUSECAF is dedicated to securing and controlling cash management activities.

Its framework covers access rights, payments, flows, banking interfaces, fraud, traceability and business continuity.

Objective: provide a clear view of control maturity and priority actions.

Banking exchanges

SECEDI

SECEDI secures exchanges, processes and transactions, including those based on EBICS TS.

The solution supports risk identification, control design, evidence verification and remediation tracking.

Objective: strengthen the security and traceability of sensitive exchanges.
