Project directors

Project director

Gilles

Gilles is a senior banking, cybersecurity and regulatory-compliance professional. He has more than 20 years’ experience in banking across France, the United States and Southeast Asia, including 12 years at HSBC in commercial banking and global banking and markets. Since 2021, he has specialised in cybersecurity, regulatory compliance, artificial intelligence and operational resilience, combining extensive banking experience with a deep understanding of regulatory and technology risk. A certified DORA Lead Manager, he regularly speaks at international conferences, including Forum InCyber in France, Luxembourg and Morocco, on operational resilience, cybersecurity and regulatory compliance in financial services and other critical industries.

He places particular emphasis on the human dimension of operational resilience. He sees training, awareness and a strong resilience culture as essential elements of an effective framework, alongside governance, technology and risk management. His expertise spans DORA, NIS2, GDPR, MAS TRM and ISO 27001, with a focus on translating regulatory requirements into concrete, understandable measures that business, technology and risk teams can apply. His work at the intersection of banking, AI, cybersecurity, regulation and human factors brings regulatory expectations closer to operational reality.

Parham

Parham Mouli is a senior leader in cybersecurity, technology, operational resilience and AI governance, with strong experience in systemically important financial institutions. His cross-functional approach connects AI, cybersecurity, technology, risk, payments, treasury, procurement and executive leadership while keeping human accountability at its centre.
