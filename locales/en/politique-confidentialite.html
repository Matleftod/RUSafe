Privacy policy

This policy explains how information submitted through the contact form is handled.

Data controller

R’U SAFE, whose legal details must be completed in the legal notice, is the data controller for information collected through this website.

Data and purpose

When you use the contact form, we process your name, organisation, job title, business email address, type of request, timeline and message. This information is used solely to respond to your request, prepare a discussion and follow it up.

Legal basis and recipients

This processing is based on R’U SAFE’s legitimate interest in responding to professional enquiries. The data is accessible only to authorised R’U SAFE personnel and, where necessary, technical service providers acting on its behalf.

Your rights

You may request access to, rectification or erasure of your data, restriction of processing or object to its processing, subject to applicable law. To exercise your rights, email contact@rusafe.fr. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data-protection authority (CNIL).

The website currently uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. No consent banner is therefore displayed. This policy must be updated before any such tool is introduced.
