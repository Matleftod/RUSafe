Our approach

Assess, remediate, automate and manage.

A seamless service model to understand gaps, execute actions, industrialise control and support informed decision-making.

One approach, four stages

Each stage produces immediately usable deliverables and prepares the next.

Assess, then act

Understand quickly, remediate concretely.

01 — Assess

Assess actual maturity and compliance levels.

We rapidly assess your framework against DORA, NIS2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, the AI Act, LPM/RGS and internal standards.

  • Critical gaps and risks
  • Insufficient controls
  • Dependencies and remediation priorities
  • Quick wins
02 — Remediate

Turn findings into completed actions.

We build realistic, prioritised action plans that teams can use immediately.

R’U SAFE can lead remediation, coordinate stakeholders and track commitments through to effective closure.

Industrialise, then decide

Executive steering is the end goal of the control chain.

03 — Automate

Structure controls and reduce manual work.

Requirements, owners, expected evidence, collection, gaps, alerts, non-conformities, remediation and dashboards are connected in one operating model.

Objective: dedicate more time to risk analysis.

04 — Manage

Consolidate Cyber, IT, Risk, AI, Compliance, Procurement and Business functions.

Executives identify residual risks, required trade-offs, priority investments and the associated accountabilities.
